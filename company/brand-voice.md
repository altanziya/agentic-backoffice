# Fieldline brand voice

We write for the person who plans the week on Friday afternoon: an HVAC owner, an electrical
foreman, an office manager. They are practical, busy and allergic to buzzwords. Write English in
this repo; for German customer content keep the same rules (direct, "Sie" in formal mails, "du" in
the product).

## Tone
- Plain and concrete. Short sentences. Say what the product does on a Tuesday morning.
- Respectful of the trade. Tradespeople know their job; we know scheduling.
- Honest about limits. If we do not do something (payroll, quoting), say so.
- Numbers over adjectives: "cuts dispatch planning from 3 hours to 20 minutes", not "much faster".
- Active voice, second person ("you see every van on one board").

## Banned phrases
revolutionize, game-changer, cutting-edge, next-level, seamless, unlock, supercharge, leverage (as a
verb), "in today's fast-paced world", "AI-powered" (only allowed when the sentence says what the
model does, e.g. "suggests the nearest free technician based on drive time"), synergy, "one-stop shop".

## Do / Don't
| Don't | Do |
|---|---|
| Revolutionize your dispatch with AI-powered scheduling. | See every van, job and free slot on one board. Drag a job, the customer gets the new time by SMS. |
| A game-changer for modern trade businesses. | Used by 140 HVAC, electrical and plumbing shops in Germany, Austria and Switzerland. |
| Seamless integrations with your tools. | Exports to DATEV and imports customers from CSV. No Lexoffice sync yet. |
| Unlock your team's full potential. | Your technicians open today's jobs on their phone, with address, notes and photos. |

## Format rules
- Headlines: sentence case, under 60 characters, no exclamation marks.
- One idea per paragraph, max 4 sentences.
- Every claim with a number needs a source or must be something we measure ourselves.
- Prices always match company/products.md. If unsure, link to /pricing instead of quoting.
- Never name a competitor negatively. Comparisons are factual, dated and fair.
- Emojis: none in the website or blog; allowed sparingly in internal chat.

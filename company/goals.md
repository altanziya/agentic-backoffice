# Goals: Q4 2026 (October to December)

Measured against the KPI data (see kpis in company.yaml). Baselines are as of the start of the quarter.

## Objective 1: Grow recurring revenue steadily
- KR1.1: MRR from about EUR 38k to EUR 46k by 31 Dec (revenue.mrr_eur, last row).
- KR1.2: New paid accounts: at least 14 per month (product.new_paid, summed per month).
- KR1.3: Monthly logo churn below 2.0 percent (product.churned / active_accounts).

## Objective 2: Fill the funnel from search
- KR2.1: Trial signups: at least 330 per month (web.signups_trial, summed per month).
- KR2.2: Visit-to-trial rate stays at or above 2.0 percent (signups_trial / sessions) in every week.
- KR2.3: Organic clicks up 25 percent over the quarter (search.clicks, 28-day windows).
- KR2.4: Move 5 quick-win queries (position 4 to 15) into the top 3 (search/queries.csv).

## Objective 3: Win the mid-size accounts
- KR3.1: Pipeline of EUR 60k open deal value in demo/proposal/negotiation at any time.
- KR3.2: No deal sits more than 7 days past its next-step date (crm pipeline overdue list is empty on Fridays).
- KR3.3: 5 new Business-plan accounts.

## Objective 4: Keep the back office honest
- KR4.1: Every scheduled agent job runs without manual re-run in at least 95 percent of runs.
- KR4.2: All agent costs inside the budget in backoffice.yaml.
- KR4.3: Website audit shows zero broken links and zero price mismatches before 15 Nov.

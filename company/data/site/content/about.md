# About us

We are seven people in Berlin building scheduling software for trade businesses. Mara and Tobias
started Fieldline after watching a heating contractor plan his week on a whiteboard that nobody else
could read.

We keep the product small on purpose: planning, dispatch and job documentation. We do not do quoting
or accounting, and we say so.

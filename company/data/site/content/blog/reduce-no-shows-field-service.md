# How to cut no-shows in field service appointments

A technician arrives and nobody opens the door. For a service van that is roughly EUR 90 of lost
time, plus the next job running late.

## Three habits
- **Confirm 2 days before.** One SMS with date, window and a reply option.
- **Remind the morning of.** A short message with the technician's first name.
- **Send an arrival window.** "Between 10:00 and 11:00" beats "sometime Tuesday".

# HVAC service scheduling checklist for the winter rush

The first cold week brings more calls than a normal month. Prepare before it.

## Checklist
- [ ] List all maintenance contracts due in October to December.
- [ ] Keep two emergency slots free per technician per week.
- [ ] Check van stock for the ten parts you order most.
- [ ] Brief customers on response times for non-emergency calls.
- [ ] Agree who answers the phone when the office is full.

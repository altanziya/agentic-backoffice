# Dispatch board vs spreadsheet

A whiteboard works with three technicians. At six, the cracks appear: double bookings, a customer
who was never called back, and one person who knows the plan.

## Five signs you have outgrown it
1. Only one person can read the plan.
2. You answer "when is the technician coming?" by phone more than ten times a day.
3. Job notes live in WhatsApp.
4. You re-type paper job sheets into the invoice.
5. Vacation planning needs a meeting.

## What to do
Move the plan to one shared board first. Add customer reminders second. Only then touch routes.

# Plan the week in 20 minutes, not 3 hours

Fieldline shows every technician, job and free slot on one board. Drag a job to a new time and the
customer gets the update by SMS. Technicians open today's jobs on their phone.

[Start your 14-day free trial](https://app.fieldline.example/signup). No credit card.

## Built for small trade businesses

- HVAC, electrical, plumbing and heat pump installers with 3 to 25 technicians
- Used by about 140 companies in Germany, Austria and Switzerland
- Hosted in Frankfurt, German and English interface

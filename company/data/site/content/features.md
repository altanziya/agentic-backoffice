# Features

## Dispatch board
See every technician and job for the week. Drag and drop, with conflict warnings for double bookings.

## Mobile app for technicians
Today's jobs with address, notes, photos and a signature field. Works offline in cellars and plant rooms.

## Customer notifications
SMS reminders and "technician is on the way" messages with a live arrival window.

## Maintenance contracts
Recurring jobs for yearly boiler and heat pump service, generated automatically (Team plan and up).

## Exports and API
DATEV export for your accountant. API and webhooks on the Business plan. Details on
[pricing](/pricing). Questions about our newest release? See the [release notes](/blog/does-not-exist).

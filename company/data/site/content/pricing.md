# Pricing

Prices per company per month, excl. VAT. No per-user fees, no setup fee.

| Plan | Price | For |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | EUR 149 / month | up to 5 technicians |
| Team | EUR 279 / month | up to 15 technicians |
| Business | EUR 599 / month | up to 40 technicians |

Extra technicians: EUR 9 per month each. Annual billing includes 2 months free.

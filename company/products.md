# Products and pricing (source of truth)

Fieldline is one product with three plans. Prices in EUR excl. VAT, billed per company per month (not
per user). Annual billing: 2 months free. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial, no credit card.

| Plan | Price / month | For | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | EUR 149 | up to 5 technicians | Dispatch board, mobile app, customer SMS reminders, job photos |
| Team | EUR 299 | up to 15 technicians | Starter + recurring maintenance contracts, route suggestions, DATEV export, customer portal |
| Business | EUR 599 | up to 40 technicians | Team + API and webhooks, SSO, roles and permissions, priority support (4 h response) |

## Details
- Extra technicians beyond the plan limit: EUR 9 per technician per month.
- Onboarding: free self-serve; guided onboarding call (60 min) free for Team and Business.
- Hosting in Germany (Frankfurt). Data processing agreement available on request.
- Not included: quoting, invoicing, payroll, accounting. We export to DATEV and import customers from CSV.
- Languages: German and English UI.

## Not offered
- No per-user pricing, no setup fee, no long-term contract (monthly cancellation).
